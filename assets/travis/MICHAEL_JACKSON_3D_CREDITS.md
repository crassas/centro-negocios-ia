## Modifications made for the Travis assistant

- Imported into Blender alongside the existing CC0-derived Travis anatomical bust.
- Cropped the model to remove most of the lower costume/torso; positioned and scaled to the existing glTF coordinate frame.
- Preserved the original Travis head and eye object names as hidden rigging/animation anchors.
- Applied a separate shader to the original licensed 3D face, retaining its diffuse facial texture and adding holographic rim, sweep, sand-grain dissolve and audio-reactive mouth deformation.
- Rendered front and three-quarter inspection frames.
- Exported editable `.blend` and deployable `.glb` files for the Travis interface.
